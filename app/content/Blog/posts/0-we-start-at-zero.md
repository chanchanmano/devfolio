I've envisioned writing a blog for such a long time, and like most things you envision in life (and maybe in tech), it needs the right excuse, and I've finally run out of ways to put it off. So I figured the time has come to break the barrier of lethargy and daydreaming by putting pen to paper or maybe finger to key.

So here it is - a small announcement of me commencing the writing of a tech blog, almost like a rite of passage in the industry, I have come to believe. But what I want to achieve here is quite simple, straightforward, and maybe even selfish.

The Computer Science Sphere of Imposters (CSSI) or what others refer to as the IT industry is ever-changing. As I write this from a desk overlooking the highway in Davis, some founder three time zones away is pitching the micro-"est" SaaS you've ever seen, built on a framework that didn't exist when I started this sentence. It has become next to impossible for a developer to stay "up-to-date" with the rapid development happening across every facet of the industry. At the same time, our collective attention spans have plummeted, no thanks to leading consumer media; you're probably itching for a Subway Surfers video to pop up on top of this as you read.

I aspire to achieve 2 things with this blog - and the second takes precedence.

Firstly, I want to share with the world my insights and takes on whatever piece of technology, code, or development I'm deep-diving into. This gives me a chance to not only explore new and old tech but also form an opinion on it , pushing ME to think instead of my Claude (shhhh! They might be listening).

Secondly, it keeps me on my toes and gives me the chance to catch up on stuff that's happening in the IT industry and try to develop a knowledge of artifacts that goes beyond the surface level. Writing about what I deep-dive into is just a layer of knowledge preservation.

I know myself well enough to see two outcomes here. Either I will keep up with this and be so consistent that you will witness my AI clone write these articles and blog entries long after I've moved over to God's crib OR I will have a total of 4 blog entries. "Build in public" is supposed to keep you consistent, I feel it is supposed to keep you afraid of societal embarrassment at a large, quite public scale. So the intention behind having an "announcement" in my network for my blog is just a way of engineering (scaring) myself into doing the right thing — being consistent.

In this blog, I am going to try to stick to technology and mostly from the world of Computer Science and Software Engineering. But you can (and should) expect an odd post about Al Pastor tacos, the San Andreas Fault, Arsenal's dream of winning the league, the Battle of Normandy or the symbolism in Francis Ford Coppola's *The Godfather*. Still, mostly tech.

So here goes nothing.

~ AH
